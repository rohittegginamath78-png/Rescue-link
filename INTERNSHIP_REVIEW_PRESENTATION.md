# Slide 4: Project Description

## Project Description

RescueLink is a wildlife first-aid and rescuer discovery platform.

It helps users:

- Get immediate first-aid guidance for injured animals
- Upload a photo of the animal for better AI context
- Find verified rescuers near their city
- Filter rescuers by specialty
- Submit rescuer details for admin review

### Speaker Notes

The project is designed for people who may be stressed after finding an injured animal. Instead of searching randomly online, users can open RescueLink, describe the situation, upload a photo if needed, and get practical first-aid steps. If the case is serious, they can quickly move to the rescuer finder.

## Key Features

- AI wildlife first-aid chat

- Rescuer finder with map view
- City normalization for names like Hubli, Hubballi, and Dharwad
- Specialty filters for birds, mammals, and reptiles
- Community rescuer submission form

### Speaker Notes

The AI chat gives calm and practical guidance. The new photo upload feature allows users to share visual context, while the AI is instructed not to diagnose with certainty. The rescuer finder shows verified rescuers and uses location or manual city selection. The admin panel makes sure submitted rescuers are reviewed before becoming public.
